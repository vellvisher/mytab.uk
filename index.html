MyTab

Effortless finance tracking


Keeping on top of your finances shouldn't feel like a second job. We built MyTab so you can see exactly where your money goes - in seconds, on your iPhone, without ever handing your data to anyone.

Snap a photo of any receipt or bill and MyTab reads the amount, date, and merchant for you - no manual typing. Every expense lands in the right account and category automatically, so your spending stays organised while you get on with your day.

Travelling? Start a trip and log spending in the local currency. MyTab converts everything back to your home currency with live exchange rates, so you always know what you're really spending.

Track balances across multiple accounts, set savings goals, and watch your progress on a clean monthly calendar. When you need a record, export a tidy PDF or CSV statement straight from the share sheet - that's where MyTab earns its keep.

What you get

  • Scan receipts & bills - capture an expense from a photo, no typing.
  • Multiple accounts & currencies - see every balance in one place, converted with live rates.
  • Travel expenses - group spending by trip and settle up in your home currency.
  • Savings goals - set a target and track how close you are.
  • Calendar & insights - spot your spending patterns at a glance.
  • Statement export - share a clean PDF or CSV whenever you need one.

Privacy policy

Your financial data stays on your iPhone. MyTab has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no hidden trackers. We never send your transactions, receipts, or personal details to any server. The only network request the app makes fetches public currency exchange rates so amounts can be converted - this request contains no personal information. Statements you export are shared only through the destinations you choose.
